# Deckroom — One-Sheet

## THE PITCH

**Private sound-treated rooms. Pioneer DJ XDJ-RX3. Book by the hour. No membership.**

Deckroom is practice space for working DJs and people learning to DJ in NYC. 
Two locations (Greenpoint, Brooklyn & Lower East Side, Manhattan). Same 
professional gear in both. Fast booking, immediate confirmation, keypad entry.

---

## THE LOCATIONS

### Greenpoint, Brooklyn
- Address: Off Manhattan Ave (G train, 5 min walk)
- Hours: 9 AM–11 PM daily
- Rate: $45/hour
- Vibe: Quiet side street. Treated room. No daytime noise limits.

### Lower East Side, Manhattan
- Address: LES (F/J/M/Z at Delancey–Essex, 4 min walk)
- Hours: 9 AM–12 AM daily
- Rate: $60/hour
- Vibe: Below street level. Natural isolation. Built for late sessions.

## THE GEAR (BOTH ROOMS)

- **Player:** Pioneer DJ XDJ-RX3 (2-channel all-in-one system)
- **Sound:** Powered monitors, tuned to the room
- **Inputs:** USB sticks, laptop over USB
- **Extras:** Booth stand, cabling, spare headphones
