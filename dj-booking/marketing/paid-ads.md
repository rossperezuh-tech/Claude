## Budget / Targeting

**Audience (Primary):**
- Age: 20–45
- Interests: DJing, music production, nightlife, Greenpoint/LES residents
- Geographic: NYC (Greenpoint, LES, surrounding neighborhoods + outer boroughs)
- Behaviors: Music event attendees, clubgoers, people searching DJ-related terms

**Budget allocation (suggested):**
- Google Search (high intent): 50% of budget
- Instagram/Meta: 30%
- TikTok/YouTube Shorts (brand awareness): 15%
- Retargeting (abandoned bookings): 5%

**Bidding strategy:**
- Search: Maximize conversions (CPA bid ~$10–15 per booking)
- Social: Conversion or Lead generation (ROAS 3:1 target)
- Retargeting: Cost-per-click (~$0.50–1.50)
