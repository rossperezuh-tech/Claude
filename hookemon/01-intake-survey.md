Send Thursday. Takes ~5 minutes. Results go straight into each person's skill card (`06-skill-card.md`) and decide station groups.

**Intro text for the form:**

---

## How to use the results

- **Station groups:** sort by total score. Mix levels within a group (one 2–3 per group helps the 0–1s), but don't put a 3 at a station they've already mastered.
- **Role weighting:**
  - Floor / sales → prioritize *Sell it* track + 30-second demos on everything.
  - Support → prioritize troubleshooting, connectivity, firmware/updates.
  - Marketing → prioritize making something good-sounding fast (content).
- **Q10 answers** → collect into a "hard questions" list and answer them live in the Q&A block.
