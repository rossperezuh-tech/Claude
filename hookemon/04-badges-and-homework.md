# Badges & Homework

A badge = one device passed at a level. You (Nick) sign off. Record it on their skill card.

## Badge criteria (same for every device)

| Level | To pass |
|---|---|
| **Operator** | Make a sound, build a short pattern or part, save it, load it back. Done without help. |
| **Performer** | Play a 2–5 min set using this device synced to at least one other device or a computer. Start, transitions, and a clean ending. |
| **Instructor** | Give the 30-second demo to a "customer" (you or a coworker) and answer two questions from the objection list in `05-sell-it.md`. |

Saturday goal: everyone leaves with **at least one Operator badge**. Demo-ready staff (rated 2+) attempt **Instructor**.

## Homework (MIDI practice)

Each person gets **one** homework task matched to a device on their skill card. It's due in 1 week. They send a 30–60 second recording (a phone video is fine).

Format for each task:

- **Device:**
- **MIDI file:** `[GEARBASE]` — the file name from the library
- **Task:** what to recreate or do with it
- **Done when:**

### Templates

**1. Rebuild the groove (Operator)**
- MIDI file: `[GEARBASE]` drum pattern, ~90–100 BPM
- Task: listen to or load the pattern, then reprogram it by hand on the device.
- Done when: it matches the timing, and you've swapped at least one sound for one you chose.

**2. Play it in (Operator → Performer)**
- MIDI file: `[GEARBASE]` chord progression or bassline
- Task: send the MIDI into the device from a computer, then learn the part and play it in live over your own drums.
- Done when: you can play it twice through without stopping.

**3. Flip a sample (Operator, samplers)**
- MIDI file: `[GEARBASE]` drum pattern, used as the backing
- Task: sample something from your day (voice, object, room). Chop it onto pads and play it over the pattern.
- Done when: the sample is on at least 4 pads and in the beat.

**4. Two-device jam (Performer)**
- MIDI file: `[GEARBASE]` full arrangement (drums + bass + chords)
- Task: split the parts across two synced devices and perform a 2-minute version with a beginning and an end.
- Done when: it's recorded in one take.

**5. The customer demo (Instructor)**
- No MIDI file. Start from a saved pattern.
- Task: record yourself giving the 30-second demo as if to a customer.
- Done when: it's under 45 seconds, makes a sound in the first 5, and ends with who the device is for.
