# Post-Session Survey

Hand out via QR code at the close. 2 minutes. This gives you the before/after numbers for the follow-up pitch to TE.

1. Name
2. Re-rate yourself on the devices you worked with today (same 0–3 scale as the intake survey).
3. How confident are you demoing TE gear to a customer now? (1–5)
4. How confident were you before today? (1–5)
5. What was the single most useful thing today?
6. What do you still feel unsure about?
7. Would you want ongoing sessions? (No / Monthly / Every couple of weeks)
8. Which product should the next session focus on?

## What to pull out for TE

- Average self-rating change per device (intake vs. post)
- Confidence change (Q3 vs. Q4)
- Badges earned in the room
- Top requests for next time (Q6, Q8)
- Homework completion rate after 1 week
