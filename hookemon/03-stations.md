# Station Guides

Every station follows the same 20-minute structure so people know what to expect:

1. **First sound (2 min)** — power on, make noise, adjust volume.
2. **Core workflow (10 min)** — the one thing this device is for. Make something.
3. **Connect it (4 min)** — sync or USB to something else.
4. **The demo (4 min)** — practice the 30-second demo from `05-sell-it.md`.

Facilitator notes: let them touch it first, explain second. Anything marked `[STEPS]` — verify exact button sequences on the current firmware before Saturday.

---

## Station A — EP-133 K.O. II (sampler / drum machine)

Good first station for beginners: it's fast to get a beat going.

- **First sound:** play the pads, switch between groups.
- **Core workflow:**
  - Program a 1-bar drum pattern (kick, snare, hat)
  - Sample something (voice, clap, phone) into a pad `[STEPS]`
  - Use punch-in effects / faders while the pattern plays
- **Connect it:** sync to another device or a computer `[STEPS]`
- **Watch for:** people getting lost between pattern/scene/group. Draw the hierarchy on a card.

## Station B — OP-XY (sequencer / synth / sampler)

- **First sound:** pick a synth track, play the keys.
- **Core workflow:**
  - Record a melody or chords into a track
  - Program drums on another track
  - Try the step components / sequencer tricks `[STEPS]`
- **Connect it:** MIDI out to another device; USB to computer `[STEPS]`
- **Watch for:** depth overwhelm. Stick to 2 tracks for beginners.

## Station C — OP-1 field (synth / sampler / "tape")

- **First sound:** pick a synth engine, play.
- **Core workflow:**
  - Record onto tape, overdub a second part
  - Sample something via the mic / radio and play it back chromatically
  - Use the tape tricks (reverse, speed) `[STEPS]`
- **Connect it:** USB to computer for file transfer; sync to OP-XY or others `[STEPS]`
- **Watch for:** people not understanding the tape model. Compare it to a 4-track recorder.

## Station D — Pocket Operators + EP-1320 / OP-Z (quick wins)

Use for whatever's in scope. Pocket Operators are great for the "anyone can do this" demo.

- **First sound / core workflow:** write a pattern, chain patterns, use the punch-in effects.
- **Connect it:** chain two Pocket Operators via 3.5mm sync and play them together `[STEPS]`
- **Watch for:** fast wins — this is where 0-rated people get confidence.

## Station E — Audio & recording (TX-6, TP-7, CM-15, OB-4)

Mostly for support + marketing roles.

- **Core workflow:**
  - Mix two devices through the TX-6 and route to the OB-4 / headphones
  - Record a short voice memo / interview on the TP-7 with the CM-15
  - Get the file off the TP-7 onto a computer or phone `[STEPS]`
- **Watch for:** routing and gain staging questions — these are the support tickets.

## Assigning stations

From the survey, give each person **3 stations**:

1. A device they rated **0–1** that's important to their role.
2. A device they rated **1–2** (move them up to demo-ready).
3. Their choice or the device customers ask about most (survey Q7).
