The Method

Seed to jar, in six steps

01

Start from a seed

Begin with good genetics — I run Laughing Buddha, a forgiving, high-yield sativa. Germinate until the seed cracks and puts out a taproot, then set it gently into the soil.

02

Grow in living organic soil

Organic soil feeds the plant for you — far less nutrient math and much harder to burn than bottled feed. It's the reason a first grow can actually succeed.

beginner-friendly
03

Top early — grow wide, not tall

My plants hit six feet, which is more than a home tent wants. The fix is to top them early: snip the main stem so the plant bushes out sideways instead of shooting up. You get more bud sites at a height you can actually manage.

04

Flip to flower before the stretch

Switch the light to 12 hours on, 12 off while the plants are still short — they roughly double in early flower. Flip on time and the canopy finishes at a height that fits the room.

05

Harvest when the trichomes turn amber

Don't harvest by the calendar — harvest by the plant. Under a cheap loupe, the frosty resin heads shift from clear to cloudy to amber. Amber is your green light.

→ → clear · cloudy · amber = cut
06

Dry controlled, cure in glass

I dry in a controlled dryer for a slow, even finish, then cure in glass jars — burping them daily. The cure is what turns dry plant matter into something smooth and worth the wait.
