# Steadyhand AI Consulting — Sales Scripts

Use these talking points for discovery calls, pitches, and emails. Customize for your prospect's specific situation.

---

## **Universal Opening Lines**

### **For Any Business Owner/Leader**
*"I work with business owners who are buried in repetitive work — document review, data analysis, proposal writing, follow-ups. I build AI tools that automate the 80% of routine work so you focus on strategy and growth. Sound familiar?"*

### **For Busy Professionals**
*"Most [title]s I talk to say the same thing: too many emails, too much manual work, not enough time for actual work. I build Claude AI tools that handle the repetitive stuff. Ever felt that way?"*

### **For Scaling Companies**
*"You're growing fast, but your processes haven't kept up. I build AI tools that scale your workflow without scaling headcount. Interested?"*

### **For Data-Driven Leaders**
*"You've got data everywhere but you're not getting insights from it fast enough. I build tools that turn your data into decisions in minutes instead of hours. Curious?"*

---

## **Discovery Questions — Find Their Biggest Pain**

Ask these to understand what matters most:

**Time & Efficiency**
- "What task or process eats up most of your week?"
- "If you could automate one thing, what would save you the most time?"
- "What are you doing manually that you wish was automatic?"

**Quality & Accuracy**
- "Have you ever missed something important (deadline, clause, metric, opportunity)?"
- "Where do errors happen most in your workflow?"
- "What quality issues cost you time or money?"

**Scaling**
- "What's limiting your ability to grow right now?"
- "If you could clone yourself, what would you have your clone do?"
- "What are you saying no to because you don't have capacity?"

**Decision Making**
- "How do you currently make big decisions?"
- "Do you have the data you need to decide confidently?"
- "What decisions take too long because of information gathering?"

**Communication**
- "How much time do you spend on repetitive communication (emails, proposals, reports, updates)?"
- "Do your messages land the way you want them to?"

## **The Pitch Framework**

Once you know their pain, show them the tool:

### **Step 1: Confirm the Problem**
*"So if I'm hearing you right, [their pain point] is eating 5+ hours per week. Is that accurate?"*

### **Step 2: Introduce the Solution**
*"Here's what I'd build: a [tool name]. You [action], and Claude [outcome]. Basically, it automates the routine work so you can focus on the thinking."*

**Examples by pain point:**

**If they said: "Contract/document review takes forever"**  
→ Document Intelligence  
*"We build a document analyzer. You upload any doc — contract, proposal, report, whatever — and Claude extracts the key info, risks, action items, and flags what needs your attention. 80% faster review."*

**If they said: "I don't understand my data / miss opportunities"**  
→ Data Pattern Analyzer  
*"We build a data analyzer. Feed it your sales data, customer data, operational logs — whatever matters to you — and Claude finds patterns, anomalies, and opportunities you'd miss manually. You'd spot 3-5 opportunities per month you currently miss."*

**If they said: "Writing proposals/emails takes too long"**  
→ Communication Accelerator  
*"We build a copy generator. You tell it what you need (proposal, email, marketing copy, announcement), and Claude writes it in your tone. Takes 5 minutes instead of 2 hours."*

**If they said: "My processes are chaotic and inefficient"**  
→ Process Optimizer + Workflow Automator  
*"We audit your current process and identify bottlenecks. Then we build automation to eliminate manual steps. Most people save 20-40% of the time in that workflow."*

**If they said: "I'm missing customer feedback / not hearing what customers want"**  
→ Feedback & Sentiment Analyzer  
*"We pull your reviews, surveys, support tickets — all feedback — and Claude extracts themes. You see exactly what customers love, what breaks, what they want. Actionable roadmap from customer data."*

**If they said: "I struggle with decisions or strategic questions"**  
→ Decision Support Assistant  
*"We build a decision tool. You present a decision plus relevant data, and Claude weighs options, surfaces risks, and recommends a path. Better decisions faster."*

## **The Close**

Once they see the value:

### **If they're interested:**
*"Great. Here's how we'd move forward: I'd put together a proposal with the exact scope, timeline, and investment. Then we do a quick kickoff call to nail down details. 50% deposit and we start next week. 3 weeks later you have a working system and we spend 4 weeks training your team. Sound good?"*

### **If they're hesitant:**
*"Totally fair. Here's what I'd suggest: let's do a quick 30-minute design session where I sketch out exactly what this would look like for you. No pressure. Sometimes seeing the actual thing makes the decision easy. Sound good?"*

### **If they say "I need to think about it":**
*"Good instinct. Let's schedule the design session for next week so you have specifics to think about. Once you see what it'd actually look like, the decision gets easier."*

## **Pricing & Timeline**

Have these ready to answer quickly:

**On Price:**
*"Depends on scope. If it's one focused tool, $4.5K. If it's 2-3 tools working together, $9K. Full suite with everything is $16.5K. Most land in the $9K range. What's your rough budget?"*

**On Timeline:**
*"One tool is 2 weeks. 2-3 tools is 3 weeks. Full suite is 5 weeks. Way faster than traditional software development because we know what we're building."*

**On ROI:**
*"If it saves you 5+ hours per week at your hourly rate, it pays for itself in the first month. Most people see 10+ hours saved weekly."*

## **Objection Handlers**

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Sounds like ChatGPT to me" | "Fair comparison, but ChatGPT is generic. This is custom-built for your specific workflow, trained on your data and processes. Way more useful than the generic version." |
| "We're not ready for AI yet" | "I get it. Most businesses felt the same way. But once you see it in action, you realize how much time it actually saves. Want to see a quick demo first?" |
| "We already have software for this" | "What software? [Listen]. OK, so it handles X but not Y, right? This fills the gaps and automates the parts yours doesn't. Actually complements what you have." |
| "This sounds expensive" | "Upfront it is. But if it saves 5+ hours per week at your rate, it pays for itself in month one. Plus, 30-day money-back guarantee. You literally can't lose." |
| "Who else is using this?" | "I work with [examples: founders, consultants, sales teams, etc.]. Can't share names for privacy, but happy to share case studies once we're further along." |
| "Can you do a free trial?" | "I don't do free trials, but the 30-min discovery call is basically that — I sketch out exactly what it'd look like, and you can decide if the investment makes sense." |
| "Our team isn't tech-savvy" | "Neither are most of my clients. But the whole point is you don't need to be — it's designed to be simple. I spend 4 weeks training your team so they're comfortable." |
| "We're happy with the status quo" | "I hear that. The thing is, your competitors probably aren't happy with it. They're already automating this stuff. You're good now, but 12 months?" |
| "How do I know it'll work?" | "You don't. That's why I give a 30-day money-back guarantee. If it doesn't save 5+ hours per week, full refund. But I'm confident it will." |
| "Can you do more features?" | "Absolutely. That's why we do a discovery call — to nail down exactly what matters to you. We customize for your specific situation." |

## **Advanced Closes — For When They're On the Edge**

### **The Timeline Close**
*"Best case, you start next week and have a working system in 3 weeks. Worst case, you procrastinate and this is still your reality in 6 months. What if we just locked it in?"*

### **The Saving Close**
*"You're spending 8 hours a week on [task]. That's 400 hours a year. At your rate, that's [$X]. We build this in 3 weeks for [$price]. Do the math."*

### **The Regret Close**
*"Six months from now, there are two versions of you: one who built this and has 10+ hours back each week, and one who didn't. Which one do you want to be?"*

### **The Competitor Close**
*"Your competitors are already automating this. Are you comfortable staying behind while they move faster?"*

### **The Risk Reversal Close**
*"Here's what I'll do: we build it. If after 30 days you don't think it's saving you 5+ hours per week, full refund. You literally have zero risk."*

## **In Meetings — Credibility Builders**

Drop these to build trust:

- "I've built this exact tool for [number] businesses now. Pattern is always the same — 5-10 hours saved weekly."
- "The cool part is, you own all the code and data. Not locked into us or any platform."
- "We deploy it locally so you don't have to worry about privacy or external dependencies."
- "Most of my clients are skeptical at first too. Once they see it in action, they're believers."
- "I've got a 30-day money-back guarantee because I'm confident this works. I stand behind it."

## **Quick Reference — What to Say When**

**When they describe their pain:**  
→ "How much time does that cost you per week?" (Get specific number)

**When they say it's low priority:**  
→ "What if I could save you 5 hours per week? Would that move the needle?" (Get real answer)

**When they ask about price:**  
→ "What's the hourly value of your time?" (Then show ROI math)

**When they ask about timeline:**  
→ "How long can you wait for this problem to be solved?" (Create urgency)

**When they seem convinced:**  
→ "So next step, we do a quick design session to nail down scope. Can we get on calendars for next Tuesday?" (Close for next meeting)

**When they go quiet:**  
→ "What concerns do you have?" (Surface objections instead of dead air)

## **The Pitch Deck Structure (If you need visuals)**

**Slide 1:** Your pain point (said back to them)

**Slide 3:** How we solve it (the tool + how it works)  
**Slide 4:** What you get (deliverables, timeline, support)  
**Slide 5:** Investment + ROI  
**Slide 6:** Next steps  

Keep it simple. Let your tool do the talking.
