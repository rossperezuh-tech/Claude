Use these talking points during discovery calls, emails, and pitches to different industries.

## **STARTUP LAWYER** ⚖️

### Opening
*"I work with startup lawyers who are drowning in contract reviews and compliance work. I build AI tools that handle the 80% of routine contract analysis so you can focus on strategy and client relationships. Curious if that's something you'd find valuable?"*

### Pain Points to Probe
- "How long does it typically take you to review a term sheet or operating agreement?"
- "Have you ever missed a clause or obligation in a doc?"
- "What's your current process for cap table reviews?"

### The Pitch
*"Here's what we'd build: an AI contract analyzer. You upload any doc — term sheet, SAFE, employment agreement, whatever — and Claude auto-extracts obligations, risks, missing clauses, and generates an action checklist. It's like having a junior associate who reviews every doc in 90 seconds."*

### The Close
*"Most lawyers save 4-6 hours per week and never miss a deadline again. We build it in 3 weeks for $9K. Want to talk through what your biggest bottleneck is?"*

## **Universal Closes**

### If they say "That sounds interesting but I need to think about it"
*"Totally fair. Here's what I'd suggest: we do a 30-minute discovery call where I walk through exactly what we'd build and you see if the timeline and investment make sense. No pressure, but if it's a fit, we can kick off next week. Sound good?"*

### If they ask about price
*"Depends on your tier. Essential is $4.5K if you want one focused tool, Hub is $9K for 2-3 interconnected tools, Enterprise is $16.5K for the full suite. Most land in the $9K range. What does your operation look like?"*

### If they ask about timeline
*"Essential is 2 weeks. Hub is 3 weeks. Enterprise is 5 weeks. We move fast because we've built these before. You'd see a working system way sooner than traditional software development."*

### If they worry about it being 'AI' and not 'real'
*"Fair question. What this actually is: Claude reads your documents or data and extracts patterns and insights faster than you can manually. It's not magic — it's trained on millions of examples and knows what to look for in contracts, proposals, resumes, etc. Think of it like having a smart junior person who learns your business in week one and handles the repetitive work. You stay in control, you review everything, but you save 10+ hours per week."*

### If they worry about data privacy
*"Your data stays local or on your infrastructure. We're not uploading anything to the cloud unless you want cloud deployment. Most clients run this locally or on their own servers. You own the code, the data, everything."*

### If they say yes
*"Perfect. Let's get you onboarded. Here's what happens next: I'll send you a proposal with the scope, timeline, and next steps. We'll schedule a 30-min kickoff call to nail down details. 50% deposit gets us started immediately. 3 weeks later, you have a working system and we spend 4 weeks training your team. Sound good?"*

## **Objection Handlers**

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "We're not ready for AI yet" | "That's fair. Most businesses weren't ready, but once they see it in action, they realize how much time it saves. Even a 30-min conversation might change your mind." |
| "This sounds like ChatGPT" | "Sorta, but way more tailored. ChatGPT is generic. This is custom-built for your specific workflow and trained on your data and templates." |
| "We already have software for this" | "What software? [Listen]. OK, so it handles X but not Y, right? This fills the gaps and automates the parts they don't." |
| "We need this in our existing tools" | "Fair point. We can integrate with Slack, CRM, email, whatever. That adds 1-2 weeks to build but it's doable." |
| "Who else uses this?" | "I can't share client names for privacy, but I work with [industry type] and [industry type]. All small businesses like yours. Happy to share case studies once we're further along." |
| "Can you do a free trial?" | "I don't do free trials, but I do a 30-min discovery call and can sketch out exactly what we'd build so you see the value before committing. And we have a 30-day money-back guarantee — if it doesn't save you 5+ hours per week, full refund." |
